# ⚖️ NyayMitra AI — Your Free AI Legal Friend

**Bringing justice to every Indian citizen through the power of AI agents.**

NyayMitra AI is a free, AI-powered legal rights assistant designed to make legal support accessible to every Indian citizen. It uses a multi-agent AI architecture to understand legal problems, research applicable Indian laws, generate legal documents, and discover government welfare schemes — all through a simple, multilingual interface.

🌐 **Live Demo:** [NyayMitra AI](https://nyaymitra.lovable.app)

---

## 🚨 The Problem

India has a significant access-to-justice gap. Millions of citizens, including daily wage workers, farmers, auto drivers, and domestic workers, face legal challenges such as:

- Salary theft and unpaid wages.
- Illegal evictions and rental disputes.
- Hospital overcharging and consumer fraud.
- Domestic violence and workplace harassment.
- Lack of awareness about government welfare schemes.

Many people cannot afford lawyers, understand complex legal terminology, or even know what legal rights protect them.

**NyayMitra AI aims to bridge this gap by making legal information and assistance accessible, understandable, and free.**

## 💡 Our Solution

NyayMitra AI is a multi-agent AI-powered legal assistant that allows users to describe their legal problems through text or voice in their preferred language.

Four specialized AI agents work together to provide relevant legal assistance:

| AI Agent | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| 📝 Intake Agent | Understands, categorizes, and analyzes the user's legal problem. |
| ⚖️ Legal Research Agent | Identifies relevant Indian laws, legal provisions, and applicable rights. |
| 📄 Document Drafter Agent | Generates RTI applications, legal notices, and formal complaint letters. |
| 🏛️ Scheme Radar Agent | Identifies relevant government welfare schemes and provides application guidance. |

## ✨ Key Features

### 🗣️ Multilingual Voice Assistance
- Supports Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, and English.
- Voice input allows users to explain their problems naturally.
- Speech synthesis reads legal guidance aloud in the user's language.

### 🤖 Multi-Agent Legal Analysis
- Four specialized AI agents collaborate to analyze legal problems.
- Identifies applicable laws and legal provisions.
- Explains complex legal concepts in simple language.

### 📄 AI Legal Document Generation
- Generates ready-to-use RTI applications.
- Creates formal legal notices.
- Drafts complaint letters.
- Provides downloadable PDF documents.

### 📸 AI Document Scanner
- Scans physical legal documents using images.
- Analyzes rent agreements, salary slips, hospital bills, and other documents.
- Highlights potentially problematic clauses.
- Explains complex document terms in simple language.

### 📊 Case Strength Meter
- Provides a visual assessment of case strength.
- Evaluates available information, evidence, and applicable laws.

### ⏳ Legal Deadline Tracker
- Calculates relevant filing deadlines.
- Displays color-coded countdowns to help users track important dates.

### 📑 Case Summary Report
- Generates professional PDF reports.
- Includes legal analysis, applicable laws, action timelines, and generated documents.

### 📲 Easy Sharing and Accessibility
- Download PDF reports and legal documents.
- Share results through WhatsApp.
- Includes a lawyer recommendation indicator to help users understand when professional legal assistance may be necessary.

### 🏛️ Government Welfare Scheme Discovery
- Identifies potentially applicable central government welfare schemes.
- Provides eligibility information and step-by-step application guidance.

### 🤝 Free Legal Aid Resources
- Provides information about free legal assistance.
- Highlights NALSA and District Legal Services Authority resources.
- NALSA Legal Aid Helpline: **1516**.

## 🏗️ How It Works

1. **Describe the Problem:** Users enter their legal issue through text or voice.
2. **AI Intake:** The Intake Agent understands and categorizes the issue.
3. **Legal Research:** The Legal Research Agent identifies relevant laws and provisions.
4. **Document Generation:** The Document Drafter Agent prepares relevant legal documents.
5. **Scheme Discovery:** The Scheme Radar Agent identifies potentially applicable government schemes.
6. **Get Results:** Users receive legal guidance, case insights, downloadable documents, and recommended next steps.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Follow these steps to run the project locally.

### Prerequisites
- Node.js and npm installed.
- An OpenRouter API key for AI functionality.

### Installation

1. Clone the repository:

```bash
git clone YOUR_GITHUB_REPOSITORY_URL
```

2. Navigate to the project directory:

```bash
cd nyaymitra-ai
```

3. Install dependencies:

```bash
npm install
```

4. Configure your environment variables if required by your project:

Create a `.env` file and add your API configuration:

```env
VITE_OPENROUTER_API_KEY=your_api_key_here
```

5. Start the development server:

```bash
npm run dev
```

6. Open the local URL displayed in your terminal.

**Note:** Never commit API keys or other sensitive credentials to GitHub. Keep your `.env` file private and configure the corresponding environment variables securely.
